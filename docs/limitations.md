# Limitations

- The selected system is a model binding cavity, not a clinical drug target.
- The initial release does not include an unbound control or a second ligand.
- Classical force fields approximate electronic structure and polarization.
- Conventional MD may not sample slow pocket transitions.
- A stable ligand pose does not demonstrate experimental binding affinity.
- RMSD and RMSF are descriptive and are not convergence proofs by themselves.
- Endpoint energy estimates, if added, will require explicit assumptions and
  must not be presented as experimental free energies.
- Findings from this single benchmark cannot be generalized to all protein-ligand
  systems without further evidence.
